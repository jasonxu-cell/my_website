Overview

Why Gofar?

The Gofar oceanic transform fault provides a natural setting for studying how earthquakes are distributed along a segmented fault system. The project focuses on the relationship between focal-mechanism variation, event location, and structural differences between fault segments.

Rather than treating the earthquake catalog as a single population, the analysis compares mechanism families and asks whether their spatial organization reveals distinct mechanical domains within the fault zone.

Current work

Comparing mechanisms across space

The current stage organizes focal mechanisms into comparable groups and uses Kagan angles to quantify their orientation differences. These measurements can then be compared with along-fault position and proposed segment boundaries.
